What this site covers

GiB covers video games worth talking about — new and old, big-budget and one-person passion projects. Posts fall into three buckets: recommendations (short lists built around a mood or moment, like a rainy afternoon or a long flight), deep dives (one game, examined properly), and guides (practical write-ups for players trying something new, like speedrunning).

Who it's for

This blog is written for players who already finish games and are looking for the next one — people tired of scrolling storefronts with four hundred nearly-identical thumbnails and no idea which one is actually worth their evening. If you want a straight answer on whether a game is worth your time, and why, you're in the right place.

How posts get made

Every game covered on GiB has actually been played through, not skimmed for a trailer and a Wikipedia summary. Opinions are personal and sometimes contrarian — GiB would rather be useful to the right reader than agreeable to everyone.

A short pledge: no sponsored placements, no review scores designed to average out nicely, and no post about a game that hasn't actually been finished.
