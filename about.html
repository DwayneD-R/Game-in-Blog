Why it's called GiB

A gibibyte (GiB) is a unit of digital memory equal to 1,073,741,824 bytes (2³⁰ bytes)—fitting, considering video games run on digital storage. However, that actually has nothing to do with how I thought about the name of the site. The name is actually a pun on the words “game” and “in” sounding like “gaming.” Since this is a gaming blog, it landed on Game in Blog, or GiB.

What this site is for

If it’s gaming related, then it belongs on GiB. From recommendations, deep dives, and practical guides to breaking news and honest reviews, anything about gaming is fair game. Whether it is a massive AAA release, an obscure indie project, or industry news, if gamers care about it, GiB covers it.

This blog is built for gamers tired of corporate game journalism and sponsored reviews. It's written for gamers who actually finish games and are searching for what to play next—people who are tired of scrolling through storefronts for hours. If you want a straight answer on whether a game is genuinely worth your time, or if you're just looking for a solid guide or news update, you're in the right place.

How posts get made

Every game covered on GiB has actually been played through, never skimmed from a trailer or a Wiki summary. Opinions are personal and occasionally contrarian, because GiB would rather be useful to its readers than agreeable to everyone.

About the writer

I'm Dwayne, a BSIT student who is passionate about gaming and wants to share his love for video games with the world. I'm a League of Legends addict who uses playing other games as a form of rehab. These days, I enjoy all sorts of games—except PvP shooters, mostly because I suck at them.

A short pledge: no sponsored placements, no review scores, and no post about a game that hasn't actually been finished.
